📦 Algorithm Explorer: Smart Packing

One problem, four algorithms.

The problem: 0/1 Knapsack

You have a container with a limited weight capacity and a list of items, each with a weight and a value. Every item can be taken once or left behind. Which items should you pack to get the highest total value without going over the limit?

Why it matters: The same pattern appears when loading a delivery truck, choosing which projects fit a budget, cutting stock material, or picking ads for limited airtime. Exact answers save money, while fast approximate answers keep large systems responsive.

What the program computes: the set of items with the maximum total value whose total weight is at most the capacity, solved with Brute Force, Greedy, Dynamic Programming and Backtracking (branch and bound), so you can compare their answers and costs.

Your input

Weights must be positive whole numbers, values must be non-negative numbers. Up to 25 items (brute force is limited to 20).
